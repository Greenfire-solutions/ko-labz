KO-LABZ

A crew game for finishing real projects together, on a 3D star map.
Every project is a star system; what it needs orbits it as planets. Your
crew helps each other's projects, earns points and badges, and keeps a
record of every bit of help in the Mission Ledger.

Everything runs on this Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere, there is no
account to make, and it needs no internet after the first start.


1. MOVE THIS FOLDER INTO APPLICATIONS

   Drag the whole "Ko-Labz" folder into Applications (in Finder: Go,
   Applications). Keep everything in the folder together. It works from
   anywhere, but from Downloads, Documents or the Desktop your Mac may ask
   for permission first.


2. START IT

   Double-click "Start Ko-Labz" (the icon with the little star system).

   The first time, your Mac may say it cannot check the developer, or that
   the app is from an unidentified developer. That is normal for a
   download. To open it anyway:

     - macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer: click Done. Open System Settings,
       then Privacy and Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway"
       next to Start Ko-Labz. Double-click it again and choose Open.
     - macOS 12, 13 or 14 (Monterey, Ventura, Sonoma): right-click (or
       Control-click) "Start Ko-Labz", choose Open, then click Open.

   You only do this once. If the icon will not open at all, double-click
   "Start Ko-Labz.command" in the same folder instead; it does the same
   thing (allow it the same way if your Mac asks).

   Ko-Labz opens in your web browser at http://127.0.0.1:8350.
   Bookmark that page. Clicking the icon again just brings the page back.


3. SET UP YOUR CREW

   The first screen asks for your crew's names and your first project.
   Four ready-made projects (a space station, an album launch, a food
   forest, a product launch) get you going in one click. Press ? on the
   map at any time to see the keys.


4. PHONES AND LAPTOPS ON THE SAME WI-FI

   To let the rest of the crew join from their own phones or laptops,
   double-click "Start Ko-Labz on Wi-Fi" instead. A window shows the
   address to type on each phone, something like:

       http://192.168.1.23:8350

   The phones must be on the same Wi-Fi as this Mac. Every screen updates
   live. The same addresses are listed inside Ko-Labz under Systems.

   If your Mac asks whether "node" may accept incoming connections, click
   Allow, or the phones cannot reach it.

   Anyone on that Wi-Fi can play and edit, so use it at home, in the
   studio or on another network you trust, not on cafe or hotel Wi-Fi.
   To go back to this Mac only, restart the Mac and use "Start Ko-Labz".


WHERE YOUR HUB IS SAVED

   In a folder in your home folder called:

       Ko-Labz Data

   (In Finder: Go, Go to Folder, type  ~/Ko-Labz Data  and press Return.)

   The whole hub is one file in there, hub.json, saved after every change.
   Ko-Labz also keeps a copy every 10 minutes in the "backups" folder
   inside it (the last 30).

   Replacing the Ko-Labz folder with a newer version never touches it.
   Deleting the Ko-Labz folder does not delete it either.


BACK IT UP

   The quickest way: in Ko-Labz, open Systems and press "Export hub". That
   saves the whole hub as one file you can keep anywhere. "Import hub", in the
   same place, brings it back (Ko-Labz keeps a copy of what it replaced).

   Time Machine (System Settings, General, Time Machine) also includes
   the Ko-Labz Data folder automatically.

   To go back to one of the automatic copies: restart the Mac, open the
   backups folder, copy the file you want next to hub.json, rename it to
   hub.json (replacing the old one), then start Ko-Labz.


STOP IT

   Closing the browser tab is fine. Ko-Labz keeps running quietly in the
   background until you log out or restart. Restarting the Mac stops it.


HELP

   If something goes wrong, the details are in:
       ~/Library/Logs/Ko-Labz
   Needs a Mac with macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer. Mac only for now.
